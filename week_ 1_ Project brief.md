## 1. The Question

> which wards in OFFA LGA, of Kwara State are more than 5km from a health facility? 

## 2. Why this question.

Healthcare accessibility is important because the location of a health facility can influence how easily people reach medical services. Residents in settlements far from healthcare facilities may face additional travel distances, especially when seeking maternal care, treatment for illness or urgent medical attention, i will be glad to carry out this project for the next 12 month.

Wards in OFFA LGA, of kwara state and its road connection.
